Privacy Policy for GiftMate

Last Updated: 1 November 2026

Introduction

GiftMate is designed with privacy in mind. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored by the application, how that information is used, and how optional AI-powered gift suggestion features operate.

By using GiftMate, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Information Stored by the App

GiftMate allows users to store gift-related information, which may include:

  • Gift ideas and gift details
  • Recipient names
  • Special occasions and important dates
  • Notes and reminders
  • Budget and spending information

How Your Data Is Stored

All information entered into GiftMate is stored locally on your device.

GiftMate does not transmit, upload, or synchronise your personal data to any external servers except when you voluntarily use the AI-powered gift suggestion feature described below.

GiftMate does not operate a cloud service and does not maintain copies of your data.

Data Sharing

GiftMate does not sell, rent, share, or transfer your personal information to any third parties.

No user data is collected for advertising, marketing, profiling, or analytics purposes.

Third-Party Services

GiftMate does not use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, cloud storage providers, or tracking technologies.

GiftMate may use a third-party AI service exclusively to generate gift suggestions requested by the user. Information submitted for these requests is used only to generate a response and is not used for advertising, profiling, or analytics purposes.

AI-Generated Gift Suggestions

GiftMate includes an optional feature that uses artificial intelligence (AI) to generate gift suggestions based on information you provide, such as interests, hobbies, age ranges, occasions, preferences, or other gift-related criteria.

When you request AI-generated gift suggestions, the information entered for that request is securely sent to an AI provider solely for the purpose of generating gift recommendations and returning them to you.

GiftMate does not store AI prompts or AI-generated responses on external servers. Any information used to generate gift suggestions is processed only for the duration necessary to provide a response.

Information submitted through GiftMate for AI-generated gift suggestions is not stored by the AI service after processing and is not used for AI model training.

AI-generated suggestions are provided automatically and may not always be accurate, complete, or suitable for every situation. Users should independently evaluate any recommendations before making purchasing decisions.

Permissions

GiftMate may request certain device permissions solely to provide app functionality, such as:

  • Notifications for gift reminders and important dates
  • Storage access where required for backups or exports created by the user

Any permissions requested are used only for their intended purpose and are never used to collect personal information.

Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, the security of that data is largely dependent on the security settings of your device. Users are encouraged to use device passcodes, biometrics, or other available security features.

Children's Privacy

GiftMate does not knowingly collect any personal information from children because no information is transmitted from the device to the developer, except information voluntarily submitted when using the AI-powered gift suggestion feature.

Your Rights

Since all data remains on your device, you maintain complete control over your information.

You can review, modify, export, or delete your data at any time within the application or by uninstalling the application from your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published within the application or through the application's store listing.
